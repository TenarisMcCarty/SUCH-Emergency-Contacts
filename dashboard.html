          Help

          How it works

          Each card opens a page with a Text All Yard Supervisors button and Call buttons. Whoever is on shift is the primary call. Published changes reach every card within a minute.

          Common tasks

          Shift rotation changed
          Shifts → tick who works each shift → Publish
          A supervisor joins or leaves
          Supervisors → Add supervisor or remove them → check Shifts → Publish
          New card
          People → Add card → Publish → Print & QR
          Lost card
          People → Remove → Publish. Only that card stops working.
          Name typo on a card
          People → Rename → Publish, then print a new card. The tag stays the same.
          Someone on holiday or out
          Shifts → Time off → Add time off → who, first and last day, who covers → Publish
          Number not checked
          Call or text the person, then Still right → Publish
          Spanish
          Fill in the Spanish boxes in Supervisors and Shifts. Blank boxes show in English.
          Access for someone new
          Ask the site owner

          Problems

          Publish fails
          Read the message under Publish. For GitHub problems, ask the site owner.
          Someone else published first
          Lock, sign in again and redo your change.
          Forgot password
          Forgot password? on the sign-in screen
          Locked by itself
          After 30 minutes without use, with nothing waiting to publish. Sign in again.
